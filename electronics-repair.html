Electronics Repair FAQ

Do you do micro-soldering?

Supported micro-soldering and board-level work can be assessed. Not every board or component is repairable.

Can you repair an HDMI port?

Supported HDMI port failures can be assessed. Console, laptop and other board designs vary.

Will every board-level repair be successful?

No. Components, traces, multilayer boards and prior repair damage can limit repairability.
