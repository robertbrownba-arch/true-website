Accessibility

OnyxTech Solutions aims to make this website usable by as many customers as possible.

Accessibility features

  • Semantic headings and landmarks.
  • Keyboard-focus indicators and skip navigation.
  • Responsive layouts for phones, tablets and desktops.
  • Reduced-motion support when requested by the operating system.
  • Labels for booking form fields and accessible status messaging.
  • High-contrast dark interface with cyan accents.
