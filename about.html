About the Technician & Business

Technology Problems Deserve a Real Solution

OnyxTech Solutions L.L.C. is a Connecticut technology service business focused on practical repair, troubleshooting, infrastructure and support.

Robert Brown

Owner / Technician, OnyxTech Solutions L.L.C.

Based in the Waterbury area, Robert built OnyxTech around a simple idea: when a technology problem is too specific for a generic help desk, customers should have a local technician who can investigate the actual hardware, software and environment.

Experience & Approach

The business combines IT support, computer hardware troubleshooting, networking, electronics repair, gaming systems, mobile devices, POS environments and data-recovery knowledge into one service relationship.

  • Diagnose before replacing parts whenever practical.
  • Explain options in plain language.
  • Protect customer information and avoid collecting unnecessary credentials.
  • Be clear about estimates, uncertainty and repair limits.
  • Refer or decline work when licensing, equipment or safety requirements exceed the available scope.

Why Choose OnyxTech?

There is no honest way to promise that one technician is “the best” for every repair. The better promise is transparent: OnyxTech aims to be the technician who takes the time to understand the problem, communicates clearly and solves what is realistically solvable.
