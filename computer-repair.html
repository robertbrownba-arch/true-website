Computer Repair FAQ

Do you repair gaming PCs?

Yes. Gaming PC diagnostics, upgrades, cooling, storage, RAM, GPU installation and supported hardware repairs are available.

Should I keep using a failing hard drive?

If the drive is clicking, disappearing or failing, stop using it when possible and ask about data recovery before replacing it.

Can you make an old laptop faster?

Often. We evaluate storage, RAM, operating-system condition, thermals and hardware health before recommending upgrades.
