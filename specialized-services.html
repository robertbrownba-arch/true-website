Specialized Service FAQ

Can every PCB be repaired?

No. Multilayer damage, unavailable components, proprietary construction and severe corrosion can make repair impractical.

Do you guarantee data recovery?

No. Recovery depends on the media condition and circumstances. A diagnostic assessment is required.

Can you repair industrial computers?

Supported industrial PC hardware can be evaluated. Proprietary systems may require manufacturer coordination.
