Stop Using a Failing Drive

  • Do not reinstall the operating system.
  • Do not repeatedly run repair utilities.
  • Do not copy new files onto the affected media.
  • Keep the original media available for assessment.

Data Recovery FAQ

Can deleted files always be recovered?

No. Recovery depends on whether the data was overwritten, the filesystem condition and the physical state of the media.

Can you recover a clicking hard drive?

A clicking drive can indicate physical failure. Stop using it and request an assessment; recovery is not guaranteed.

Can SSD data be recovered?

Sometimes, but SSD architecture, TRIM, encryption and controller failure can make recovery difficult or impossible.
