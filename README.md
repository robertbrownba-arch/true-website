# OnyxTech Solutions L.L.C. website

Production-oriented static website for OnyxTech Solutions L.L.C., designed around local search, service discovery and lead generation while preserving the dark blue/cyan/gold technology aesthetic.

## Included
- Dedicated service pages for computer repair, phone/tablet repair, gaming systems, data recovery, networking/cabling, POS/printers, virus/software, electronics, business IT, smart-home/security technology, audio, sales, maintenance and specialized services.
- About page describing Robert Brown, credentials and service philosophy.
- Service-request form with anti-spam CAPTCHA enabled through FormSubmit.
- Starting-price estimator and strong call/email/booking CTAs.
- Local SEO titles/descriptions, canonical URLs, sitemap and robots.txt.
- LocalBusiness structured data on the homepage.
- Responsive navigation, keyboard focus states, skip link and reduced-motion support.
- Privacy and Terms pages.
- GitHub Actions static quality check.

## Launch checklist
1. Verify the FormSubmit destination email and confirmation behavior.
2. Deploy the site to `https://www.onyxtechsolutions.info/`.
3. Verify HTTPS, redirects, sitemap and robots.txt.
4. Connect Google Search Console and submit the sitemap.
5. Add the real business address/service-area details to schema only when they are confirmed and appropriate to publish.
6. Add verified Google Business Profile review content only after obtaining permission and current reviews.
7. Run Lighthouse and mobile/browser QA after deployment.
8. For paid advertising, use dedicated landing URLs and conversion tracking rather than claiming organic pages themselves are equivalent to paid ads.

## Important
The website can generate leads and provide paid-ad landing pages, but it cannot guarantee Google, Yelp or Yellow Pages ranking or ad performance. Advertising accounts, budgets, targeting, tracking and platform policies determine paid placement.
