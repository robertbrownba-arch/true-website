Phone Repair FAQ

Do you repair iPhone and Android phones?

Supported iPhone, Android and tablet models can be assessed. Parts and repair options vary by model.

How much does a screen replacement cost?

It depends on the exact device. Request service with the model number for a current estimate.

What should I do after water damage?

Power the device off, disconnect it from charging and avoid repeated power-on attempts. Request an assessment.
