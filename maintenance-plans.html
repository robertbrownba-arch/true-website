Maintenance FAQ

Do I need a maintenance plan for one computer?

It can make sense for heavily used or business-critical systems, but a one-time tune-up may be more appropriate for some customers.

Can a maintenance plan guarantee no downtime?

No. Preventive maintenance reduces avoidable problems but cannot guarantee uninterrupted operation.

Do you monitor backups?

Backup review and agreed monitoring tasks can be included depending on the plan and tools used.
