Frequently Asked Questions

Repair & Service FAQ

Answers to common questions before you call or submit a request.

Do you offer same-day service?

Availability depends on workload, service type, parts and location. Call for current availability.

Do you guarantee data recovery?

No. Data recovery depends on media condition, controller behavior, encryption and other factors. We provide an assessment before committing to a scope.

Do you work on gaming consoles?

Yes, supported console repairs include troubleshooting for power, HDMI, storage, overheating and other common faults.

Do you repair phones?

Yes, supported phone and tablet service includes screen, battery, charging and diagnostics, subject to model and parts availability.

Can I get an exact price online?

Many repairs require diagnosis. The site provides starting estimates only; final pricing is confirmed after the scope is understood.

Do you provide on-site service?

On-site service is available for eligible jobs and locations. Travel and service conditions may affect pricing.

Do you handle business POS and printers?

Yes. OnyxTech supports POS, self-checkout, receipt printers, scanners and related retail technology where access and service arrangements permit.

Do you need my password?

Do not submit passwords through the website. If access is required, we discuss a secure method appropriate to the job.
